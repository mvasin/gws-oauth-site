Privacy Policy

This Google OAuth application is used solely by its owner for personal command-line automation via the Google Workspace CLI (gws).

Data Access and Storage

  • Access is limited to the owner's own Google account and the scopes explicitly granted during local OAuth login.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted locally on the owner's machine and are never uploaded to any third-party server.
  • No Google user data is collected, stored externally, sold, or shared with third parties.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account Security settings.
